How to Play

Objective

Clear all cells without detonating any mines. Use logic and deduction to identify where mines are hidden.

Controls

  • Left Click: Reveal a cell
  • Right Click: Flag/unflag a suspected mine
  • Middle Click: Chord click (reveal surrounding cells if flags match number)
  • R Key: Reset game
  • F Key: Toggle flag mode

Numbers

Numbers indicate how many mines are in the 8 adjacent cells. Use this information to deduce mine locations.

First Click Protection

Your first click will never be a mine - the board adjusts to ensure a safe start.
